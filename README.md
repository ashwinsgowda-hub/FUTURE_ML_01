 📈 Store Sales & Demand Forecasting System

An end-to-end time-series forecasting solution built with **Python** and **Facebook Prophet** to predict 12-week future revenue trends and generate actionable business recommendations for inventory management, cash flow planning, and category prioritization.

---

 🎯 Executive Summary & Key Results

* **Model Architecture**: Facebook Prophet (Weekly Aggregation)
* **Validation Performance**:
  * **MAE (Mean Absolute Error)**: ~$7,293.68 / week
  * **MAPE (Mean Absolute Percent Error)**: 45.90%
* **Key Business Takeaway**: Successfully stabilized high daily volatility by converting daily transactions to weekly blocks, allowing clear trend detection for upcoming Q1 planning.

 Business Recommendations

Based on the 12-week forecast trends generated by the model:

1. **Inventory Optimization**: Direct upcoming procurement budget toward high-growth product categories (e.g., Technology) to prevent stockouts during demand spikes.
2. **Promotions & Clearance**: Apply targeted pricing strategies or bundling on plateauing product categories (e.g., Furniture) to keep inventory turning over and optimize cash flow.
3. **Operational Planning**: Align working capital and store staffing allocation with the identified weekly demand seasonality.

🛠️ How to Run Locally


2. **Install dependencies**:
